# Stock and witness outage on the November fixes, 3 to 5 November 2026

bench-stock asked whether the three limits imbu-prj's witness-kel-growth-2 found are gone on the stack built to remove them: issuance slowing from about 16,000 credentials in one issuer identifier, an Observer that could not recover from a gap past about 18,600 registries, and issuance slowing with the square of a witness outage (`~/code/bakobo/heti/.ignored/witness-kel-growth-2-findings.md`). It ran three times, on two stacks, all on kelun as in `docs/targets-2026-10-rerun.md`, each under a named `systemd-run --user` unit at nice 19 with `LimitNOFILE=524288`, with every role's resident memory sampled every 5 minutes (`scripts/rss.sh`). The method and its changes are in `this.i` 7wvv745c and its children. bench-stock-2 ran the 40,000 fill and its window once more on 5 November, on the stack imbu-prj's store-limits-2 built from what that window found (`this.i` 8ujhdv9k); its answers replace the last two rows below, and the earlier text is kept as the before.

## The answers

| limit | answer | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| issuance slowing past about 16,000 in one KEL | **Gone.** The fill ran at 1.47 to 1.63 a second in every 2,000, witnessing 39,992 of 40,000, and imbu's stages were flat throughout. On 5 November it witnessed all 40,000 at 1.46 to 1.61 a second. | the 40,000 fills, 4 to 5 and 5 November |
| witness memory growing with one KEL | **Gone.** No witness went above 272 MB, against 7.9 to 8.4 GB before. | the same |
| issuance slowing with a witness outage | **Gone.** With one witness of three down for 25 minutes, issuance held the offered 0.35 a second in every 10 minutes, and latency did not rise. | the outage run, 4 November |
| the Observer's recovery past 18,600 registries | **Recovers.** Restarted with 40,000 registries in the stack, it was ready in 1.70 s and took the Registrar's next full batch 1, 40,100 registries, with no gap and none missing. Before store-limits-2 it never restarted. | the 5 November window |
| a full window after a 40,000 fill | **Completes.** 176 of 180 witnessed and published, at a p50 of 0.40 s requested to witnessed; the 4 lost were sent while the issuer was down. The issuer resumed 32.0 s after it was killed, where before it never did, and no store filled. | the same |
| the stores keripy sizes, at about 40,000 events | **No longer a limit on the bench.** Its witnesses now open their stores through heti at 16 GiB, and none logged a `MapFullError`. | the same |

## The 40,000 fill

`stock-preseeded-40k-1home-observer` is `stock-preseeded-40k-1home` with one fault added: the Observer is killed at 300 s into the window and restarted at once. Every Observer start is a gap in heti, so its restart asks the Registrar for a full batch 1. At this stock that is the resync that was permanent past 18,600 registries, which a fill with no gap never exercises (`this.i` 7wvv745c).

### First, on imbu eda942c6 and heti 70c7d8e

From 21:26 UTC on 3 November the fill ran at 1.49 to 1.71 a second in every 2,000 up to 24,000. One bucket, the one starting at 10,000, ran at 1.06. No process went above 410 MB resident. At 24,681 credentials imbu's credential registry store (`imbu/h0/bench/keri/acdc/reg/lockbox/data.mdb`) reached 104,853,504 bytes. That is keripy's fixed LMDB map size, `LMDBer.MapSize = 104857600` (`keri/db/dbing.py:372`), and neither heti nor imbu then overrode it. Every later issuance failed with `lmdb.MapFullError`, 15,319 of the 40,000, and imbu reported it under the unregistered code `e.self.unknown.code.f`. The window then failed for another reason. The host's root disk filled, from other sessions' builds rather than this run, which used 706 MB. The harness died about 830 s into the window and its timeline was cut mid-line. That window has no report and is not counted (`results/stock-2026-11-03`, `this.i` 7ltuxtd8). imbu-prj fixed the map size as heti-lmdb-mapsize, and the fill was run again on that.

### Then, on imbu e912ec4b and heti fc25249

heti fc25249 maps every store heti opens through `heti.anchored.stores` at 16 GiB, unless `HETI_LMDB_MAP_SIZE` says otherwise, and imbu e912ec4b pins it (`this.i` 5nvt7d4g). The Registrar stayed at bakobo/witness 6b3f2792. The fill ran from 22:52 UTC on 4 November, and the preseed ended at about 06:00 on 5 November with 39,992 of 40,000 witnessed. 8 failed with a dropped connection to imbu (`ServerDisconnectedError`), and their cause was not traced. The fill rate never fell below 1.47 a second, where the fills of 2 and 3 November fell from about 16,000 and 20,000 respectively and were stopped at 20,466 and 23,973. Peak resident memory over the whole fill was 272 MB for each witness, 272 MB for the Observer and 566 MB for the issuer; in the fill of 2 November one witness reached 7.9 GB. At 40,157 credentials the issuer's store held 518 MB, 12.9 KB a credential, on the line the October and November runs drew. Its registry store held 170 MB, past the map that stopped the first run.

**So one issuer identifier now holds 39,992 credentials at a constant cost per credential, in time and in memory.** That is the per-identifier limit lifted to about 40,000. It is not a pass of the brief's 40,000 exactly: 8 issuances failed, each a dropped connection rather than a slowdown, and the window that was to measure issuance at that stock was refused (below).

### Its window, and the stores keripy still sizes

The window failed. The report refuses it, because the Observer's restart could not be applied. Three things happened in it. The second has a known cause; the first and third are not fully explained.

- **The Observer could not restart.** Its lockbox Baser holds the same issuer KEL as the witnesses and is as large, 104,853,504 bytes, but heti opened it with a 16 GiB map, so it was not full and logged no `MapFullError` (store-limits-2 read its meta pages; an earlier draft of this report inferred otherwise from the file's size). Restarted at 300 s, it asked each witness for its cursor query, then fetched the issuer's whole KEL through the issuer's OOBI, and that fetch timed out (`e.env.oobi.timeout.r`). It never announced itself within the harness's 600 s, so the harness stopped it, and from then on the run had no Observer. Whether the fetch was slow because of the KEL's size, about 40,000 events, or because two witnesses were then minutes from filling, is not established.
- **Two of the three witnesses' stores filled next.** At 06:07:52 and 06:08:35, about 380 and 420 s into the window, `witnesses/keri/db/wit0` and `wit2` reached the same 104,853,504 bytes, and each witness logged `lmdb.MapFullError` in keripy's `processEscrowUnverWitness`. With toad 2 of 3, one witness that can still write cannot witness anything. From about 600 s imbu's operations failed with `e.state.pending.witness.r`, then timed out at 60 s, and 57 of the window's 180 credentials were witnessed, all before then.
- **The issuer could not restart.** Killed at 900 s with an act short of its witnesses, it logged "the issuer facet has an act short of its witnesses, so 40050 untracked registries wait for a later startup to join the pool". It did not announce itself within 600 s, though the process was alive and at 440 MB, so the harness stopped it too.

40,000 events of one issuer's KEL take about 100 MiB in a Baser, about 2.6 KB an event, so every Baser opened at keripy's default reaches its map at about that size. heti fc25249 sets the map size while heti's stores open, which covered the Observer's lockbox. The bench's witnesses run `keri_bench/stacks/witnesses.py`, which copies heti's demo `Witnesses.start` loop and opens its Basers through keripy's `habbing`, so they stayed at 100 MiB. keripy's Baser reads `KERI_BASER_MAP_SIZE` when it opens (`keri/db/basing.py:935`), so a deployment can size it without a code change. Whether bakobo/witness's production witnesses set it was not checked here.

Before the kill, the Observer held 33,832 registries and no gap, its last batch number 28 arriving at 05:53:54. The Registrar then held 40,049 heads, and 38,103 credentials had been published by 900 s before that batch. So the Observer was some 4,000 registries behind what the Registrar had published, without any gap. Why was not established. heti 70c7d8e lets the Observer hold a paged batch until its last page arrives, waiting up to its resync interval, 2,700 s here, which is one possible cause. Nothing in its log names one.

### Again, on imbu bb6ac45 and heti a830fcd (store-limits-2)

imbu-prj's store-limits-2 traced the three failures of that window (`~/code/bakobo/heti/.ignored/store-limits-2-causes.md`) and fixed each. heti a830fcd opens the demo witnesses' Baser, Keeper and Mailboxer through `heti.demo.witnesses.open_witness_stores`, and setupWitness's Reger in `Witnesses._doers`, at heti's 16 GiB map. It also stops an Observer's start waiting on a refresh of an issuer it already holds, and asks a witness that is behind the held head only for its tail, not for the whole KEL. imbu bb6ac45 pins that heti and stops an issuer's restart walking every retained credential for each unfinished issuance. bakobo/witness 9c8e673 sizes the production witness's stores (`WITNESS_LMDB_MAP_SIZE`) and runs the Registrar here. The bench's own witnesses copy heti's start loop, so keri-bench 913ea19 makes that copy call `open_witness_stores` too; a test checks that all four of each witness's maps come up at heti's size (`tests/stacks/test_witnesses.py`). The scenario, harness settings and host are as before.

The window ran from 19:09:09 to 19:38:59 UTC and the report accepts it, with no unexpected process exit:

- The Observer, killed at 300 s, was ready 1.70 s after its restart and the next credential succeeded 8.6 s after that. Its start was a gap, so it resubscribed, and 736 s later, at the Registrar's next 900 s window, it received batch 1. That batch held 40,100 registries, which is every registry published by then. The Observer's store afterwards records batch 1 with no gap and none missing, and all 40,100 registries without a gap (`stores.txt`). So the resync that was permanent past about 18,600 registries now completes at 40,100. Its lockbox Baser held 119 MB, past the 104,853,504 bytes at which a 100 MiB map is full.
- The issuer, killed at 900 s, was ready 32.0 s after the harness killed and restarted it, and the first credential to succeed after that was witnessed 36.2 s after it was ready. The 4 credentials requested while it was down were refused by the proxy in front of it with a 502 (`e.party.imbu.refused.f`), as a request to a dead issuer is meant to be, and are the window's only losses. The first three after it came back took 9.4, 19.1 and 28.3 s to be witnessed, and every one after that took under 0.5 s.
- No store filled. No role logged a `MapFullError`, and with toad 2 of 3 every credential the issuer accepted was witnessed, 40,176 in one KEL, past the point near 40,000 at which two witnesses filled before.

The witnesses hold the same KEL as the Observer's lockbox, so they should be about its 119 MB. The first calibration of this run failed its interval check: one interval's p50 was 3.4 ms off against a 3.2 ms tolerance, at host load about 3 from other sessions. The harness refuses a run on a failed calibration, so it was run again, and the second passed. Host load was 2.9 when the window opened and 3.6 during it. Every role ran at the pinned commits, as the provenance records.

## The witness outage

`office-day-3homes-witness-outage` is `office-day-3homes` (0.35 a second at three issuers, every 101st credential delivered, verified and revoked) with `witness_pool.w0` killed at 3,600 s and restarted 1,500 s later. Its window is cut to 9,000 s: an hour of load, the 25-minute outage, then 65 minutes of recovery (`this.i` 7wvv745c). It ran on imbu e912ec4b and heti fc25249, from 19:47 to 22:37 UTC on 4 November, at host load 1 to 4 (`results/outage-2026-11-04`).

| | before the outage | during | after |
|---|---|---|---|
| credentials requested | 1,261 | 525 | 1,364 |
| requested → witnessed, p50 | 0.437 s | 0.423 s | 0.378 s |
| p90 | 0.923 s | 0.845 s | 0.481 s |
| p99 | 1.84 s | 1.52 s | 1.00 s |
| max | 2.71 s | 3.30 s | 1.29 s |

**Passes: issuance did not slow at all during the outage.** All 3,149 witnessed of 3,150 requested, at 0.348 to 0.352 a second in every 10-minute bucket, the outage included, with no stage failure and no unexpected process exit. Before heti-dead-lane, one dead witness took the anchor rate from 39 a second to 2 in 25 minutes, and a live fill from 1.66 to 0.19 a second. The restarted witness was ready 2.36 s after the harness restarted it, at the end of the 1,500 s outage, and the next credential succeeded 0.94 s after that; the report's time to ready, 1,502 s, counts from the kill and so includes the outage. The issuer's resident memory stayed between 100 and 111 MB, and every witness under 280 MB. All 32 sampled revocations were recognised by the verifier. Revocation requested to published took p50 0.95 s, and the slowest revocation was recognised 911 s after it was requested, inside target 3's 1,800 s. Reconciliation lists 3,118 credentials as lost; that is the lifecycle sample, since only the sampled credentials are verified. The run of record lists 9,901 of 10,001 the same way.

## What this does not show

- **More than one restart of each, or a window longer than 30 minutes.** On store-limits-2 the Observer's resync past 18,600 and the issuer's restart at 40,000 were each measured once.
- **The witnesses' store sizes at 40,000,** which teardown removed (~5thx).
- **Why the first three credentials after the issuer's restart took 9 to 28 s.**
- **Anything past 39,992 in one KEL.** With every Baser sized, the next measured limit would come from imbu ~2556, one snapshot past the Observer's 64 MiB queue, at about 70,000 registries.
- **Why, on 4 November, the Observer held about 4,000 registries fewer than the Registrar had published**, with no gap. On 5 November its batch 1 held every registry published by then.
- **The cause of 8 preseed failures** of 40,000, each a dropped connection to imbu.
- **A longer outage, a second dead witness, or an outage at the peak rate.** The outage run is one 25-minute outage at the office day's rate. Below toad, issuance is meant to stop, and that was not tested.
- **Repetitions.** Each run ran once.

## Reproduce

```
uv sync --extra stacks
systemd-run --user --unit=keri-bench-stock -p LimitNOFILE=524288 --nice=19 \
    -E PATH="$PATH" -E HOME="$HOME" --working-directory="$PWD" \
    scripts/targets.sh runs/stock results/stock scenarios/targets/<name>.toml
systemd-run --user --unit=keri-bench-stock-rss --nice=19 --working-directory="$PWD" \
    scripts/rss.sh keri-bench-stock "$PWD/runs/stock/rss.log" 300 6000000 8000000
```

Each results directory holds the run's gzipped timeline, provenance, report and `imbu-answers.json` where the run produced them, the memory and load logs, the store sizes at the end, and `SHA256SUMS` over them all.
